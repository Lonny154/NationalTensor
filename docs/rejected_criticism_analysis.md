## What the failures show

- **Missing historical citations:** the original P05 and newer P03 responses name E003 without its evidence ID. This is a model-output defect under the frozen requirement. A global citation list can also contain the right experiment ID but the wrong observation key, as in the original P05 E004 compiler claim; the validator does not reliably establish claim-level support.
- **Rounded and contextual numbers:** P02, P03, P04, P05, and P06 rejections include prose numbers such as `1.0`, `0.98`, `0.997`, `1`, or `95` that the frozen validator cannot tie to literal cited values. Some are genuinely unsupported by the response's cited keys; P06's earlier four-key rounded range is arithmetically reproducible but still invalid under the unchanged rule. The new P06 `95` describes interval confidence convention, which is present in the source's `bootstrap_median_ratio_95pct` field name but not as a literal cited scalar. These are different failure modes; the common first-error label alone does not judge scientific correctness.
- **Incomplete key coverage:** the newer P03 cites only a subset of the shapes it summarizes; newer P05 cites two screening records while describing all intervals; newer P06 cites four records on two shapes while listing eight medians across four. The v1 global-reference design and broad numeric matching can miss these prose-to-key gaps after a different first error stops parsing.
- **Prior measurements versus new hypotheses:** E005 measures Hilbert-versus-row-major or Hilbert-versus-grouped-M4 screening, not P03's grouped-versus-raster, P04's Stream-K, P05's persistent stationing, or P06's platooned dispatch. A model may reasonably discuss mechanism overlap but cannot transfer a measured null or gain to those proposals. E003/E004 compiler equivalence is scoped to their compared sources, not new code.
- **Screening and causal limits:** E005 screening is unconfirmed; its pipeline lacks validated profiling. Several responses acknowledge that boundary, but broad phrases such as “no profiling” should stay pipeline-scoped. L2 reuse, physical CTA order, SM idle time, and weight-slab residency remain unmeasured for these proposals. A model's hardware explanation can be useful as a testable hypothesis without becoming an observation.

The validator protects against unbound historical identifiers and many invented numbers, but its prose-token approach is neither a complete scientific reviewer nor a reliable rounding calculator. Its global citations do not ensure that every field is supported by the right observation key. Conversely, a source-grounded percentile label or explicitly reproducible rounded range can fail because the literal token is absent from the cited scalar values. These are **validator limitations**, distinct from model errors such as omitting available citations or asserting a broader experimental contrast than was actually run. The attempt collection is small, selected, and not random; **11/13 is an observed rejection count for these requests, not a population failure-rate estimate**. Rejected outputs still contain useful experimental suggestions, including Stream-K overhead isolation, P05 slab occupancy modeling, and P06 matched locality profiling; those suggestions remain human interpretation of ineligible model text.

## Experiment 009 consequence

The frozen run has **two bound eligible accepted v1 criticisms (P01, P02), four unbound proposals (P03–P06), and two accepted decisions (P01-A, P01-B)**. No P02–P06 decision exists. The minimum to obtain all twelve decisions in this run is **14 further live requests** if every future request validates: four fresh accepted v1 criticisms for P03–P06 and ten A/B decisions for P02–P06. Completion is **not possible using only the current attempts** because P03–P06 have no accepted or validly revalidated v1 criticism. A rejected criticism cannot silently enter Condition B: the frozen binding loader requires an accepted audit or explicit valid revalidation with exact source and prompt hashes. Substituting the Experiment 011 v3.1 P06 criticism would change the treatment and fail that v1 provenance rule. A future rejected decision would occupy its run/condition slot and could force a new run, so all failed requests must stay in the accounting.
